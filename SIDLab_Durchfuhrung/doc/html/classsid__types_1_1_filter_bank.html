Detailed Description

A series connection of filters.

There are 8 possible "routes" a voice's signal may take through the three SID filter types:

  • No filtering at all
  • Lowpass only
  • Bandpass only
  • Highpass only
  • Lowpass, then bandpass
  • Lowpass, then highpass
  • Bandpass, then highpass
  • Lowpass, then bandpass, then highpass

This implements all 8 routes, making it possible to switch between them "on the fly"
